How these numbers are counted
  • Applicants are unique people from the Leads tab of the Recruitment Campaign Leads 2026 sheet, matched by email (then phone). Repeat applications count once, at the furthest stage reached. No names, emails or phone numbers are published on this page.
  • Qualified or beyond = Qualified + Interview + Offered + Hired, open or closed. Reached interview = Interview + Offered + Hired.
  • Weekly charts and the before/after table use the application date, which the sheet records from 30 Sep 2026 onwards.
  • Paid spend counts from 1 Aug 2026: Meta (recruitment ad account), Google Ads (Betterhomes account, campaigns with career or recruit in the name) and LinkedIn Ads (campaign group Bh-Recruitment-Traffic-LPV-UAE-Aug-2026, USD converted at 3.6725).
  • Cost per qualified (paid ads) divides all paid spend by qualified applicants tagged as Meta ads, Google ads or LinkedIn.
Data refreshes twice a day from the leads sheet, Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn (Supermetrics) and PostHog.
